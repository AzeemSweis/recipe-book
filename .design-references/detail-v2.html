Herb Roasted Chicken

Prep

15m

Cook

45m

Total

1h

Serves

4

Cuisine

American

Made

shopping_basket Ingredients

lightbulb Chef's Notes

"Best served with roasted root vegetables and a crisp white wine. Let the chicken rest for 10 minutes before carving to keep it juicy."

cooking Instructions

1

Preheat oven

Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Lightly grease a roasting pan or large oven-safe skillet.

2

Season chicken

Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Rub the olive oil, minced garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper all over the chicken and inside the cavity. Place the lemon halves inside the cavity.

3

Roast for 45 mins

Place the chicken breast-side up in the pan. Roast for 45-50 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) and the skin is golden brown and crisp.
