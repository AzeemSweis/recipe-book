Herb Roasted Chicken

star star star star star_half (128 reviews)
Prep 15m
Cook 45m
Total 1h
Serves 4
Cuisine American
Made 12 times
shopping_basket

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (approx. 4 lbs)
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved
grocery

Instructions

1

Preheat oven

Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Prepare a roasting pan with a rack or a large cast-iron skillet.

2

Season chicken

Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Rub olive oil all over the skin. Season generously with salt, pepper, and rosemary. Stuff the cavity with garlic cloves and lemon halves.

3

Roast for 45 mins

Place chicken in the oven and roast until the skin is golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C). Baste occasionally with pan juices.

4

Rest and serve

Transfer to a cutting board and let the chicken rest for 15 minutes before carving to keep the juices locked in.

Chef's Notes

"Best served with roasted root vegetables and a crisp white wine. If you have extra time, let the seasoned chicken sit uncovered in the fridge for 2 hours for even crispier skin."
